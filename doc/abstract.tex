\enquote{Majas matematiska äventyr} (\texttt{maventyr}) is a text adventure,
in the spirit of the classic \texttt{adventure} game, that gamifies learning
across the Swedish school curriculum --- not only Swedish and mathematics but
the sciences, the social sciences, languages, and the other subjects.  The
world generates itself as a labyrinth, persists between runs, and adapts its
difficulty along the curriculum.  Tasks are graded by subject-specific graders
(\texttt{sympy} for mathematics) and authored according to the research
literature on subject didactics.  This document is the literate source: the
program and its rationale are developed together.
